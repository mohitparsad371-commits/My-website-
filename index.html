Frequently Asked Questions

Some common questions people ask before starting their journey with me.

Do you work only in Delhi NCR or pan India?
I serve clients across India. Most work (GST, ITR, registration, advisory) can be handled completely online through WhatsApp, email and calls.
What documents are needed for GST registration or ITR filing?
Documents depend on your profile (individual / business). Generally PAN, Aadhaar, bank details, address proof and business proofs are required. I will share a simple checklist once you contact.
How do you charge — monthly or per service?
Both options are available. For regular compliance (GST, TDS, accounting) monthly retainership works best. For one-time work (registration, trademark, single ITR), fixed service-wise fees are shared in advance.
Is my financial data kept confidential?
Absolutely. Your data, documents and login details are handled with full confidentiality and are never shared with anyone.
